Here's a summary of the video:

**"Knockout Sleep Tea" — The Concept**

The creator shares a nightly drink routine taken 2 hours before bed, which they claim dramatically improved their sleep quality. The core idea is that poor sleep is often a _chemistry problem_, not a willpower problem — your body needs specific conditions (dropping core temperature, rising GABA, falling cortisol) to enter and stay in deep sleep.

**The Recipe (3 ingredients + 1 supplement)**

- **Chamomile tea** — contains apigenin, which binds GABA receptors and signals the nervous system to relax
- **Glycine powder** (~10g) — an amino acid that lowers core body temperature, a key trigger for deep sleep
- **Electrolytes** (half a packet) — addresses a lesser-known issue: cells not retaining water properly, which causes people to wake up mid-night needing the bathroom
- **Magnesium glycinate** (taken alongside, not in the tea) — the most bioavailable form of magnesium; activates the parasympathetic nervous system and deepens sleep

**Why Not Just Melatonin?**

Melatonin only signals _when_ to sleep, not _how well_ you sleep. It doesn't fix temperature regulation, GABA activation, or fluid balance — so you may fall asleep faster but still miss out on deep, restorative sleep.

**The Ritual Matters Too**

Doing this at the same time every night trains your nervous system to associate the routine with sleep onset. Over time, just making the tea starts the wind-down before you even drink it.

**The 14-Day Challenge**

Try it nightly for 14 days and watch for: fewer mid-night wake-ups, and how you feel in the first 30 minutes after waking. A sleep tracker (Oura, Whoop, Apple Watch) is recommended to measure the difference objectively.
