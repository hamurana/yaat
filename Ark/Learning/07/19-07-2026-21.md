## The Hook & Core Thesis
When a tailor asks "which side do you dress on?" (left, right, or straight) before measuring your inseam for custom trousers, it's not to add extra room — it's simply to **avoid surprises** during measurement. A light, novelty-style video explaining the tailoring reason, some stats, and five (partly tongue-in-cheek) theories for why men hang one way or the other.

## Key Arguments Checklist
- **The tailor's question is practical, not flattering.** Contrary to what many men assume, no extra fabric is added on that side — the tailor just needs to know before measuring the inseam.
- **Hanging to one side is normal.** The whole point of the video is that no one tells men this is normal, and asking is treated as a joke rather than answered seriously.
- **There's no definitive scientific answer** — the host searched medical journals, Reddit, and forums and found only theories, some serious and some clearly jokes.

## High-Yield Assets
- **Why the video exists** (two stated reasons): (1) nobody tells you it's normal — he had to ask a doctor at 16 who just said "that's perfectly normal, don't worry"; (2) anyone asking the question gets made fun of, so no serious answer exists online.
- **Stats cited in the video** [attributed to the World Health Organization and his own research — presented as the speaker's cited figures, not verified]: **75% of men hang left, 17% right, 8% straight** — so 92% hang to one side, which he argues is normal. He links the 8% who "hang straight" to shorter length, and cites a length distribution (~3% around 2.75 in, up through ~75% around 5.9 in, ~15% around 7 in, ~3% around 7.87 in), arguing that longer length makes hanging to a side more or less necessary given how clothing is cut.
- **The five theories** for why men hang one way (he invites viewers to vote, and treats several as jokes):
  1. **The left testicle hangs lower**, leaving more room for the penis to bend that way without resistance.
  2. **Right-dominant men move their right leg more** (running, jumping, sitting), so keeping to the left is more comfortable in tight clothing.
  3. (Joke) "Right hand operates the mouse, left hand operates the penis."
  4. (Joke) "Penises are magnetic" — most men keep change in their left pocket, so it hangs left.
  5. **You hang in the direction you habitually tuck it**, developing a curve over years.

## Case Studies / Anecdotes
- **His custom-clothier background**: he used to own a custom clothing business and asked every client which side they dress on — the practical origin of the whole video.
- **The doctor visit at 16**: asking why he curved one way and being told simply that it's normal — his motivating example for why this basic reassurance should just be public information.

## The "So What?" Factor
If a tailor asks which side you dress on, answer plainly — it's a routine measurement question, not a comment on you, and hanging to one side (which the vast majority of men do) is entirely normal. The "why" has no settled answer; the theories range from the plausible (asymmetric testicle height, dominant-leg movement) to jokes, so treat it as trivia rather than science.
