## The Hook & Core Thesis
A house-by-house rundown of the summer/hot-weather men's fragrances worth owning — favouring bright citrus, aquatic, and coconut-driven scents that hold up as you sweat, while steering you away from the darker "dress-up" flankers that get overpowered in heat.

## Key Arguments Checklist
*(Organised by fragrance house; picks and the reasoning behind each.)*
- **Prefer citrus / aquatic / coconut for heat.** These read as "warm weather," project well as you sweat, and stay easy to wear. Dark, heavy, rich scents (elixirs, "le parfum" flankers) are generally better for winter or cool summer nights.
- **New-release convenience matters.** A brand-new fragrance is easy to find and slip into your wardrobe — a recurring reason he favours recent releases.
- **Match strength to setting.** Some scents (e.g. the [[La Nuit de L'Homme]] EDP) are intimate "skin scents" that shine up close but get overpowered at the pool; club scents you can over-spray and they survive being thrown in the pool.
- **Sample before committing on the polarising ones.** Anything unusual (the Valentino purple flanker) — test on your own skin first; body chemistry changes how it wears.

## High-Yield Assets
*(Corrected brand spellings; garbled ones flagged.)*
- **Giorgio Armani — Acqua di Gio Parfum Intense** (top pick from the house). A throwback to the 1990s original: darker, harder into citrus, less oceanic. Notes — green apple, bergamot (top); clary sage, marine (mid); wood, amber (base). Modern, clean, higher longevity/projection. Also still recommends the **Profumo** line (EDP, Parfum, and Profumo Lights) and the **Acqua di Gio Parfum**.
- **Chanel — Allure Homme Sport Eau Extrême** (easiest reach; citrus with a creamy note, ~15 years old). Top summer pick: **"Super Ligeria"** [transcript garbled — likely a Chanel Allure flanker; unresolved], a crisp well-blended citrus he calls a masterpiece, his go-to for two summers. Budget option: an older (~20-year) **Chanel "lower clone"** [transcript garbled] — more one-dimensional but a great summer citrus. Chanel's headline **"Les Exclusifs"** release is a beautiful deep, rich night-out/dress-up scent, not an everyday summer wear.
- **House of Honor [as-heard] — "Bigger It" [as-heard]**, the scent he's wearing: captures the Amalfi coast — fresh clean citrus, greens, aquatics; "citrus grove after rain with a bit of ocean."
- **Mancera [transcribed "Mabasus"]** — "Wondrous Flight" [as-heard] for a strong fruity-citrus top; **Passion Island** for something sweeter/fruitier.
- **House of Deston [as-heard] — Light Breeze [as-heard]**: lighter but tropical, citrus with aromatic spice; versatile "dumb reach." Comparable to **Parfums de Marly Percival** [transcribed "personal"] or **Chanel Égoïste Platinum**.
- **Dior — Homme Sport** ("totally slept on," often at discounters — sophisticated dress-up scent) and **Homme Cologne** (more citrus). Notes that **Sauvage** EDT is the world best-seller but he'd pick the EDP for summer; the **Sauvage Elixir** (dark, licorice, "modern masterpiece") is winter / cool-night only.
- **YSL — MYSLF EDP** (covers all bases, compliment-getter, though some find it not masculine enough — YSL leans unisex). **La Nuit de L'Homme EDP** — a slept-on intimate skin scent for getting up close. **Kouros** if you want something aggressively masculine (but not really a summer scent).
- **Jean Paul Gaultier — Le Beau Paradise Garden** (top summer pick): mint + ginger set it apart; coconut, fig, tonka, sandalwood give a tropical feel — "green salty coconut with a splash of mint." Extremely long-lasting ("get thrown in the pool and still smell like it"). Avoid the dated original **Le Mâle**; the **Elixir** is too dark/heavy for summer.
- **Prada — Luna Rossa Ocean** (top summer pick: good longevity/projection, smells good as you sweat, a no-brainer). **Luna Rossa Carbon** (original) is the most versatile and great when sweating. Avoid **Luna Rossa Le Parfum** and the new **Carbon EDP** for summer — darker/heavier, more fall/winter.
- **Valentino — Uomo Born in Roma "Purple Melancholia" [as-heard]**: polarising, sample first — lavender makes it masculine and strong, coconut makes it fun; rich, creamy, tropical, mysterious. Easier alternative: the original **Uomo Born in Roma**, solid for summer evenings.
- **Versace — Man Eau Fraîche** (a classic "cool drink on a hot day," great budget dumb-reach); **Versace Pour Homme** (simpler, the safe office hot-weather scent); **Eros Energy** (bright, fun club scent — patchouli/musk/oak moss base kept fresh by black currant; lemon-lime-grapefruit opening).
- **Dolce & Gabbana — Light Blue** (a "compliment monster" everyone's smelled) — step up to **Light Blue Eau Intense**, ~twice as strong.

## The "So What?" Factor
For hot weather, reach for bright citrus, aquatic, and coconut scents that project through sweat, and save the dark elixirs for cool nights. If you want a shortlist of easy, hard-to-go-wrong summer grabs across price points: **Versace Man Eau Fraîche** (budget), **Prada Luna Rossa Ocean**, **Acqua di Gio Parfum Intense**, and **Jean Paul Gaultier Le Beau Paradise Garden** for something fun and coconut-tropical — and always sample the polarising picks on your own skin before buying a full bottle.
