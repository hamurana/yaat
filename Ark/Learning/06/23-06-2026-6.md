**The Honest Framing**

Nothing is truly passive from day one. Every income stream on this list requires upfront effort. The passive part comes after the systems are built — and even then, they need occasional maintenance. The goal isn't effortless income; it's income that can eventually earn while you're doing other things, decoupling money from your direct time.

**1. High-Interest Savings & Index Investing**

The most boring but most accessible entry point. High-yield savings accounts (online banks) currently pay around 4–5% annually versus 0.01% at traditional banks. For investing: S&P 500 or all-world index funds have historically returned 8–10% annually over 10–20 year periods. Invest $1/day automatically; over 20 years at 7% return, ~$365/year grows to nearly $15,000. The goal at this stage isn't getting rich — it's building the habit and feeling how passive income works.

**2. Print on Demand**

Design and sell custom products (t-shirts, mugs, posters, calendars) with no upfront inventory. Platforms like Gelato handle printing, packaging, and shipping per order. You never hold stock. Key success factors: pick a niche aligned with your interests (you'll be creating content around it), trial many designs and see what lands. One seller's mug design reportedly sold thousands of units and generated $4M+ in sales.

**3. REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts)**

Stocks in companies that own large portfolios of real estate. Legally required to pay out 90% of profits as dividends — so you receive rent income without owning or managing a property. Exposure to thousands of properties with professional management. Dividend yields typically 2–6%, plus potential stock price appreciation. Accessible with as little as $50–100 on most platforms. Good diversification addition to a portfolio.

**4. Affiliate Marketing**

Recommend products you already use; earn a commission when someone buys through your link. Lower barrier to entry than most other monetisation methods — you don't need a large audience. Amazon Associates is the most common starting point (1–3% commission per sale). The strategy: create content (newsletter, blog, TikTok) in a niche you know well, link to products you actually use. Some early videos still earn small commissions years later with zero maintenance.

**5. Digital Products**

Create once, sell unlimited times. PDFs, guides, e-books, video courses — no printing, no shipping, pure margin after creation. Downsides: market is saturated (you're competing globally, not locally); copying is easy on platforms like Etsy; some buyers undervalue digital products. Upsides: zero fulfilment cost per unit, geographic freedom, no inventory. Start simple — package a solution to a specific problem you've already solved. Etsy has built-in traffic for beginners.

**The Key Takeaway**

Even earning your first $500 in passive income after a year of effort is worth it — that's $500 you didn't trade your time for. The real value at the start is building skills, understanding how markets work, and creating systems. Some streams will work; some won't. The goal is to start somewhere and build.
