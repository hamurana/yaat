**The Hook & Core Thesis**

Three years on from his last EDC video, the host has pared his everyday carry down to only what he truly needs — a small set of high-quality, intentional pieces chosen for longevity over quantity. What you carry, and why, reveals how you live.

---
**Key Arguments Checklist**

- **Quality and longevity over matching aesthetics.** He won't swap a superbly functional item just for brand consistency.
- **Buy well-made essentials once.** High-quality pieces used daily for years still look near-new — the recurring proof point.
- **Maximize an item's lifespan.** He skips yearly upgrades to get full value and actually notice the difference when he switches.
- **Minimal but not dogmatic.** He keeps pockets light but carries a purpose-built bag, packing extras as the day requires.
- **Function can carry meaning.** Several items double as sentiment (wedding band, a ring marking his early creator days).

---
**High-Yield Assets — the carry**

- **Phone:** [[iPhone 13 Pro]] (3 years old), planning to upgrade at the iPhone 17 for camera/performance, in a **[[Rimowa]]** aluminum + shockproof TPU case (microfiber-lined, signature grooves) bought at the San Francisco store while getting a luggage tag hot-stamped.
- **Card holder:** **[[Hermès Calvi]]** in Epsom calfskin — one folded piece of leather, single snap, holds 6–8 cards without bulk; fits an **AirTag** (after nearly losing a previous wallet in a hotel couch); no car key needed thanks to a Tesla.
- **Watch:** **[[Rolex Submariner No Date]]**, ref. **124060** — 41mm steel, Cerachrome bezel, 300m water resistance, Glidelock clasp, ~3-day power reserve; in the line since **1953**; gets ~80% of his wrist time even when traveling with other watches. Also owns a Rolex Oyster Perpetual.
- **Bracelet:** **[[Tiffany & Co. HardWear]]** (small link), bought at the 5th Avenue landmark flagship before Thanksgiving (gifted exclusive store-shaped Christmas ornaments); design inspired by a 1971 unisex bracelet. His wife wears the micro link version so they complement without matching.
- **Rings:** a simple domed wedding band, plus a hammered white-gold ring from a past brand collaboration marking his early content-creation days. He sticks to silver-tone jewelry for cohesion.
- **Sunglasses:** the newest addition, bought in Japan — light-blue see-through lenses with UV protection that work indoors/outdoors and don't create a "barrier" in conversation (people can still make eye contact).
- **Bag system:** a functionality-based bag collection (commuting, travel, errands); occasional extras include a camera, water bottle, and eye drops (after recent LASIK).

---
**Case Studies / Anecdotes**

- **The lost-wallet scare** — a previous wallet wedged deep in a hotel couch is what convinced him to switch to a card holder that fits an AirTag.
- **The Tiffany flagship visit** — the pre-Thanksgiving trip to the 5th Avenue landmark store with his wife, and the exclusive store-shaped ornaments, anchor the bracelet's personal meaning.
- **Japan trip** — brought three watches but wore the Submariner 80% of the time, and picked up the see-through sunglasses there.

---
**The "So What?" Factor**

Treat everyday carry as a reflection of values, not an accessory haul. Choose a few genuinely well-made essentials, use them for years, and let function (and sometimes sentiment) decide what stays — not branding or novelty. Keep it minimal but practical: carry a purpose-built bag and pack only what the day actually needs.
