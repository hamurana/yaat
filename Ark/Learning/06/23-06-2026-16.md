**The Problem with a Large Collection**

Antonio Centeno (Real Men Real Style) accumulated over 1,000 fragrance bottles — stopping counting past 700. The decision to sell nearly all of it came down to four practical problems:

**1. Disorganisation and unwieldiness**  
Even with organised systems and family help, the collection became unmanageable. Bottles used for video reviews get misplaced; things don't get put away. At a certain size, a collection stops being enjoyable and becomes a burden.

**2. Too much to realistically use**  
At 1,000+ bottles, he calculated 443 years of fragrance — assuming no new acquisitions, which wasn't realistic given companies sending 10–20 new bottles monthly for review. The collection was growing faster than it could ever be used.

**3. Capital tied up**  
Even at a conservative $50/bottle for 400 bottles, that's $20,000 sitting on shelves rather than working as investments.

**4. Degradation and perspective loss**  
An opened fragrance degrades as air enters the bottle — more use means faster degradation. And owning hundreds of bottles detaches a reviewer from the practical perspective of the average man who just wants 3–10 reliable fragrances. Constraints help keep advice relatable and useful.

**The Broader Lesson**

The video is primarily a promotional piece for a resale platform (Recented), but the underlying insight applies broadly: collections that grow beyond a certain threshold tend to invert — they own you more than you own them. The best collection is one that's used, not one that fills a room.
