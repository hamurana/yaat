**Eight Steps to Summer Style**

**1. Find the right fabrics**  
Polyester and cotton trap heat. Summer-specific fabrics breathe and cool naturally: linen (versatile, builds a wardrobe foundation), seersucker (textured, carries color well, evokes the classic Southern gentleman), Egyptian cotton, and tropical wool (lighter than standard wool; suited to warm-weather suiting). Build a base around linen shirts in solid colors and stripes, seersucker jackets and trousers, and linen-cotton-wool blend suiting.

**2. Embrace color**  
Most men hide in navy and black year-round. Summer is the time to experiment: light blue, pink, yellow, light grey, turquoise, dark green. Pastel tones work particularly well with fair complexions. Linen and seersucker carry color more vividly than synthetic fabrics.

**3. Roll up long sleeves rather than going short-sleeved**  
Very few men look good in short sleeves without significant arm development. A long-sleeved shirt rolled to the elbow maintains better proportions, looks more elegant, and allows a jacket to work over the top.

**4. Open collar; experiment with collar types**  
Skip the tie in summer, or if wearing one, go textured — knitted silk or linen blend. Try: classic button-down, spread collar, Cuban/camp collar (guayabera-origin), polo, or buttonless polo (works well with a jacket for business-casual or dates).

**5. White trousers**  
Between Easter/Memorial Day and Labor Day, white chinos, white linen trousers, or white jeans are an excellent option. Elegant and nonchalant, they pair with navy jackets, seersucker, or monochrome looks. Practical considerations: watch where you sit, use a napkin, require more frequent dry cleaning.

**6. Suede loafers**  
Leather loafers can be uncomfortable in heat. Suede loafers (e.g., blue suede) are versatile, lightweight, and work from day to evening. Break them in with socks before wearing sockless. Invisible no-show socks are not recommended — they constrict circulation.

**7. Accessories**  
Swap the brown/black leather belt for braided leather or braided cotton (particularly for white trousers). Experiment with a daring/ribbon belt in suede for a preppy look. One or two bracelets on the opposite wrist from your watch. Replace a metal watch bracelet with a NATO strap (as Sean Connery famously did with his Submariner).

**8. Summer fragrance**  
Summer scents should be fresher, aquatic, citrusy, or lightly floral — distinct from heavier winter fragrances. Recommended classics: Creed Aventus, Cool Water by Davidoff (affordable), Acqua di Parma Colonia (unisex, worn by Cary Grant, Frank Sinatra).

**Shorts Rule**  
Acceptable for daytime; hem must never fall below the knee. Inseam of 5–9 inches. Shorts are strictly daytime — switch to trousers for evenings out.
