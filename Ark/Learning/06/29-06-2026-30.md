**The Hook & Core Thesis**

The hidden driver of chronic inflammation isn't your occasional treat — it's the cheap, high-**[[Omega-6]]** oils in everyday eat-out food (外食) and repeatedly-fried "old oil." **[[Dr. Lai]]** (賴醫師), a radiation oncologist, argues this has pushed the body's Omega-6:Omega-3 balance from an ancestral 1:1 to a catastrophic ~20:1 — quietly fueling inflammation and even creating fertile "soil" for cancer.

---
**Key Arguments Checklist**

- **Cheap cooking oil is the real culprit**, not the odd indulgence — it's in nearly every eat-out meal.
- **Omega-6 vs Omega-3 balance is everything** — "fire-starters" vs "firefighters"; modern bodies have far too much Omega-6.
- **Fish oil alone won't fix it** — Omega-6 hogs the shared conversion pathway, so Omega-3 can't get through.
- **Reused fryer oil is worse than lard** — heat + oxygen degrade it into toxic compounds; "legal" oil can still be ~20–25% degraded.
- **Chronic inflammation is pro-cancer** — it builds the blood supply tumors hijack and blinds immune surveillance.
- **Defend by temperature** — no single "god oil"; use the right oil for cold dishes, daily cooking, and high heat.
- **Don't swing into health anxiety** — chronic stress harms you as much as bad oil; happiness itself heals.

**High-Yield Assets**

*Oil classification:*
- **Saturated fatty acids (飽和脂肪酸)** — lard (豬油), butter, coconut oil; solid at room temp ("flat Lego bricks stacking tightly"). Natural ≠ heart-safe: excess, plus refined carbs and inactivity, raises **LDL** ("bad cholesterol") and cardiovascular risk.
- **Monounsaturated (單元不飽和 / Omega-9)** — olive oil (橄欖油), camellia/tea-seed oil (苦茶油); raises **HDL** ("good cholesterol") and clears arterial junk ("a steel bar bent once").
- **Polyunsaturated (多元不飽和)** — the body can't make these = **essential fatty acids**; split into **Omega-3** and **Omega-6**.
- **Omega-6** — the inflammatory "fire-starter," genuinely needed for injury/immune response, but vastly oversupplied today; high in **soybean oil (大豆油), corn oil (玉米油), sunflower oil (葵花油)**.
- **Omega-3** — the anti-inflammatory "firefighter"; the reason people take **fish oil (魚油)**.

*The ratio collapse:*
- Hunter-gatherer ancestors: **Omega-6:Omega-3 ≈ 1:1**, the balance our DNA evolved for.
- Agricultural era: slight shift toward more Omega-6.
- Industrial era (last ~100 years): **soared to ~20:1**. Cause: chemical-solvent extraction of cheap plant oils, and animals fed corn/soy instead of grass — so the **Omega-3 once in meat nearly vanished**.
- **The shared-pathway trap:** the liver converts oils into vessel-protecting compounds via one route — **desaturase enzymes (去飽和代謝酵素)** — and the ~20× Omega-6 "traffic jams" it, so even expensive fish oil can't squeeze through.

*Old / reused frying oil:*
- Plant oil is **not a "free pass" (免死金牌)** — its double bonds make it unstable at high heat. In ~10+ minutes, **thermal oxidation (熱氧化劣變)** produces harmful compounds (aldehydes / trans-fat-like substances).
- All-day frying and next-day reuse cause **thermal polymerization (熱聚合劣變)** — oil turns into sticky, cell-damaging compounds. Tell-tale signs: dark oil with a persistent dense foam.
- **Taiwan FDA (食藥署) red line:** total polar compounds (**TPC, 總極性化合物**) must hit **25%** before a batch is legally replaced — but a shop may change at ~20%, so even "legal" oil already carries ~1/5–1/4 accumulated toxins.
- **Analogy:** you'd never pour black, never-changed engine oil into your car, yet people happily eat old fryer oil into the body — "a no-driver supercar."

*The cancer link:*
- Clogged vessels are only the early warning. Long-term degraded oil → chronic inflammation → the body thinks it's perpetually injured and **builds new blood vessels (angiogenesis)** that tumors hijack as a supply line.
- Chronic inflammation floods the **immune "radar" with interference**, letting cancerous cells that should be destroyed slip past — the "malignant soil" in which the cancer "seed" grows.

*The three-oil system (defend by temperature):*
1. **Cold dishes (涼拌):** **flaxseed oil (亞麻仁油)** — packed with Omega-3 but expensive, heat- and light-sensitive; cold use only; buy in dark glass, refrigerate after opening.
2. **Low–medium-heat daily cooking (中低溫炒菜):** **olive oil (橄欖油)** — rich in Omega-9 and antioxidant polyphenols, structurally stable, vessel-protective.
3. **High-heat frying (高溫煎炸):** when olive oil can't hold up, use high-smoke-point, low-Omega-6 oils — **camellia/tea-seed oil (苦茶油)** or **avocado oil (酪梨油)**. *(But minimize fried food regardless.)*
- **Formula:** cold → flaxseed; stir-fry → olive; high heat → camellia/avocado; and avoid dark old fryer oil when eating out. "Oil isn't inherently good or bad — the wrong thing is the temperature and usage."

**The "So What?" Factor**

Swap your kitchen oils by temperature — flaxseed for cold dishes, olive for everyday cooking, camellia or avocado for high heat — and steer clear of the dark, repeatedly-used oil in cheap eat-out food. That single change helps restore the Omega-6:Omega-3 balance and reopens your body's defenses. But the closing point matters just as much: don't tip into extreme health anxiety. Spend ~70% of your effort on eating well and leave 20–30% to relax — chronic stress is as damaging as bad oil, and "happiness itself is the strongest healing." Medicine treats disease; only your daily habits build real health.
