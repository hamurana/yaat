**The Disappearing Skill**

45% of men aged 18–25 have never approached a woman in person. 62% of Gen Z men have removed themselves from the dating market entirely. The video argues this reflects a broader collapse in a fundamental masculine skill: direct action — going after what you want rather than engineering conditions for it to come to you.

**The Reacher Trap**

Jack Reacher represents a dominant male fantasy: hyper-capable, masculine, desirable — and entirely passive romantically. Female characters make the first, second, and third move. The fantasy is that you become so ideal that the life you want materialises without you having to risk rejection. The problem: this keeps you in the passenger seat of your own life, waiting to be chosen rather than choosing.

**The Daredevil Alternative — Reading Signals**

Matt Murdock (Daredevil) is offered as a counterexample. He initiates, but reads the situation first. Attraction doesn't jump from strangers to kissing — zoologist Desmond Morris identified ~12 sequential steps in *Intimate Behavior* (hand, shoulder, face, kiss). Moving stepwise means you're reading her the entire way: she leans in, you move forward; she pulls back, you stay. The physical connection grows gradually so that each step feels natural rather than like a leap.

**Practical Dating Structure**

Skip dinner across the table on first dates — go somewhere with movement (dancing, a walk) where you can make early physical contact. Sit catty-corner if dinner happens. When she's reaching for you, don't respond passively — match her energy and lead one step further.

**Flirting: The Two Ingredients**

Effective flirting requires both:
- **Playfulness** — lightness, teasing, creating fun
- **Forwardness** — directness, building sexual tension, letting her know you're interested

Men who are all playful and never forward become the funny friend — she doesn't even know he's interested. Men who are only forward come across as intense or boring. The balance (Craig Ferguson examples: direct compliment immediately followed by self-deprecating humour to diffuse the tension) keeps energy fun while moving the relationship toward romance.

**The Broader Application**

The same dynamic applies beyond dating. Much of what's sold to men — get jacked, get rich, get status — is a passive strategy: become ideal and wait for life to arrive. The alternative is to get clear on what you actually want and take one direct, attuned step toward it. Clarity of desire is what enables direct action. Without it, you can only wait to be chosen.
