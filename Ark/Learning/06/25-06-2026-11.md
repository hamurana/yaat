## The 9 Rules

**1. Flip flops (away from water)**
Acceptable at a pool or beach. Not at the grocery store, restaurant, or walking around town. Rule: if there's no water in sight, leave them at home. Alternative: espadrilles, loafers, or leather sandals.

**2. Wrong fabrics**
Heavy cotton and synthetic polyester don't breathe. Fast fashion summer pieces are almost always polyester. The cheat code: choose linen, seersucker, Egyptian cotton, or lightweight tropical wool. Fabric choice does more for summer comfort than any other decision.

**3. Shorts after sunset**
Shorts are acceptable during the day for casual situations and heat above 90°F. The evening is a more formal time — swap shorts for trousers after dark. Some restaurants enforce this; a gentleman holds the standard himself regardless.

**4. Shorts below the knee**
Long shorts (11–13 inch inseam) throw off proportions — making you look shorter and legs skinnier. Stick to 7 or 9 inches (hem falls just above the knee). Swimwear at 5 inches is also fine.

**5. Winter footwear with summer outfits**
Black leather dress shoes don't belong with shorts or summer clothes. Footwear is seasonal. Loafers, espadrilles, and leather sandals all work where oxfords don't.

**6. Sports sunglasses outside of sport**
Wrap-around plastic frames are made for cycling, tennis, and active sport — not poolside or lunch. When no sport is being played, choose classic styles: tortoiseshell wayfarers, silver aviators, timeless shapes from brands like Ray-Ban, Persol, or Moscot.

**7. Sports jerseys outside the stadium**
Sports jerseys are for watching sport at the game. Wearing one while running errands sends a signal of negligence and lack of intentionality. Every piece of clothing communicates something about who you are.

**8. Sunglasses indoors or after dark**
When you wear sunglasses indoors, you can see someone's eyes but they can't see yours — this creates an uneven power dynamic and people find it offensive. Always remove them inside or after sunset. Fold them into your top pocket.

**9. Dirty white trousers**
White linen trousers in summer are elegant — but only if immaculate. Stained or poorly maintained white clothing is worse than not wearing it. If you wear white, commit to dry cleaning and maintain a high standard of cleanliness. Check the chair before sitting, always dine with a napkin on your lap.

## The Underlying Principle

Summer is actually a man's most stylish season when done correctly. The failures all come from the same root: heat and casualness make men default to convenience over intention. The solution isn't accepting heat or abandoning comfort — it's choosing the right fabrics and silhouettes that handle heat while still projecting self-respect.
